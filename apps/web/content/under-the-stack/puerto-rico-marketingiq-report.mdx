# I Audited 50 Puerto Rico Websites. Here's What the Data Says.

A comprehensive marketing technology audit across 8 industries, 8 scoring categories, 45+ analysis modules, and 50 production websites in Puerto Rico.

**Key findings:**
- Average MarketingIQ score: 76.2 (median 77.5)
- Strongest categories: Performance & Experience, SEO & Content, MarTech Infrastructure
- Weakest categories: Paid Media (38.1), Brand (41.2), Market Intelligence (42.1), Analytics (44.8)
- 98% of sites had zero A/B testing infrastructure
- 36% had single-platform or nonexistent paid media presence

The full interactive report includes radar charts, heatmaps by industry, and the top 10 most common gaps across all 50 scans.
